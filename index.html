About this portfolio

This e-portfolio collects my work on the Intelligent Agents module (IA_PCOM7E, July 2026 cohort). It is organised by unit and holds my discussion posts, wiki contributions, exercise outputs, the team design proposal and my reflections, written with Rolfe et al.'s (2001) What? So what? Now what? framework. Each artefact is linked to the module learning outcomes below. New units are added as the module progresses.

Learning outcomes and where they are evidenced

OutcomeDescriptionEvidence so far
LO1Identify and critically analyse agent-based systems, differentiating between architectures and approaches.Units 1–3 Collaborative Discussion 1; Unit 4 wiki (layered architectures); Unit 6 team design proposal
LO2Apply and critically evaluate intelligent agent techniques to real-world problems, particularly where technical risk and uncertainty is involved.Unit 2 peer responses (failure cases and safeguards); Unit 6 KQML/KIF experiments; Unit 8 ambiguity experiments
LO3Deploy critically appropriate software tools and skills for the design and implementation of an agent-based system, bearing in mind applicable legal, social, ethical and professional issues.Unit 6 and Unit 8 Python notebooks; Unit 6 team design (tools, models, APIs); Unit 2 response on chatbot liability
LO4Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be an effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment.Unit 6 team design proposal and tutor feedback; peer engagement in Collaborative Discussion 1
