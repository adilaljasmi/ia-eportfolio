Feedback received

  • Tutor (Unit 11 implementation): excellent integration of planning, retrieval, verification and blackboard coordination, with strong testing and evaluation against targets. To improve: test more scenarios, run a larger independent evaluation with statistics and comparisons, and simplify dense slides (details).
  • Tutor (Unit 6 team report): outstanding domain knowledge and strong use of literature. To improve: add a class diagram, set measurable evaluation targets and define fallback rules. I acted on all three in Unit 11 (details).
  • Peer evaluation I gave: I completed the module’s peer evaluation template (1–5 scale, with written justification for extreme scores). I recognised my teammates' contributions: one for essential input and high-quality work, the other for leading meetings well, delivering on time and producing the overall design and outline.
  • Peers (team): after I shared the draft, a teammate thanked me for the effort and said I had done a great job. At the end, the team thanked each other for the effort and commitment (from the team chat, anonymised).
